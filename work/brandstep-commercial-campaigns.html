Overview

At Brandstep I edit and lead the production of commercial videos for the agency’s clients: product films, ad creatives and social content built to stop the scroll and sell.

Featured here is work for Zubi Candy, a confectionery brand, including product films for its jelly lolly and teddy jelly lines. More client campaigns will be added as assets are cleared for public use.

Objective

Make each product look as appealing as possible in the first seconds, communicate the offer clearly, and deliver ad-ready versions for each platform on deadline.

My role

  • Advertisement editing
  • Product storytelling and pacing
  • Visual presentation and colour
  • Creative execution with the team
  • Handling client feedback and revisions

Creative approach

Product first: lighting, texture and motion that make the product the hero of every frame.

Short, clear structure (hook, product, offer, call to action) cut in versions for each placement, then refined through client feedback rounds.
