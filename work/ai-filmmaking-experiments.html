Project 03 / AI filmmaking R&D

AI Filmmaking Experiments

Cinematic visuals and short video experiments made with generative AI, exploring how far AI tools can be directed like a real camera crew.

Overview

These experiments are where I test new AI tools and techniques before using them on client or documentary work.

Each piece is treated like a film shoot: a concept, a look, a shot list, then prompting and iteration until the frames feel directed rather than generated.

Objective

Build a reliable, repeatable AI filmmaking workflow with consistent characters, intentional camera movement and cinematic light.

My role

  • Concept and shot design
  • AI video prompting
  • Image-to-video workflows
  • Character consistency
  • Edit and finishing

Creative approach

Start with strong still frames, then animate them. Camera moves, lens and lighting are written into every prompt the way a cinematographer would brief a crew.

Characters are kept consistent through reference images and a fixed description, so the same person survives from shot to shot.

Production process

  1. ConceptA mood, a story beat or a technique to test.
  2. Look developmentReference frames for palette, light and lens.
  3. PromptingShot-by-shot prompts with camera and lighting direction.
  4. Image-to-videoAnimating approved stills into motion.
  5. IterationFixing continuity, faces and motion artefacts.
  6. EditAssembling, scoring and grading the final piece.
