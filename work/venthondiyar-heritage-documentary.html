Project 01 / AI-assisted documentary

Venthondiyar Clan Heritage Documentary

An AI-assisted historical heritage documentary that brings a family’s 250+ years of history to the screen.

Overview

The Venthondiyar Clan documentary tells the story of one family across more than two and a half centuries. Very little of that history exists as photographs or film, so the visual world had to be rebuilt.

Using research as the source of truth and AI image and video generation as the camera, the film reconstructs places, people and moments that could not be filmed, held together by narration and a consistent cinematic look.

Objective

Preserve a family’s history in a form future generations will actually watch: a cinematic documentary that feels grounded and respectful rather than synthetic.

My role

  • Research-based visual direction
  • Scene-by-scene planning and shot design
  • AI image and video generation
  • Narration and editing workflow
  • Colour, pacing and final edit

Creative approach

Every scene started from research, not from a prompt. Historical details decided costume, architecture and setting before any image was generated.

A fixed visual language (palette, lighting direction, lens feel) was defined up front and reused across every generation so the film reads as one piece instead of a collage of AI clips.

Production process

  1. ResearchGathering the family history, names, places and periods.
  2. Story structureShaping the history into chapters and a narration script.
  3. Scene planningBreaking each chapter into scenes and shots.
  4. Visual developmentGenerating key images and locking the visual style.
  5. Image-to-videoAnimating approved frames into cinematic shots.
  6. Edit & narrationCutting to the voice-over, adding music, sound and grade.
