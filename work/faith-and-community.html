Overview

Alongside agency work, I create for my faith community, where the goal is participation and togetherness rather than sales.

The featured project is MULTITUDE: The Youth Carol Clash, a youth carol-singing competition presented by St. Joseph Youth, Selaiyur (Tambaram Vicariate). The name draws on Luke 2:13, the multitude of the heavenly host praising God.

Objective

Give the event a memorable identity and promotion that brings youth groups across the vicariate to take part.

My role

  • Event naming and identity
  • Poster and promotional design
  • Promotional video
  • Community storytelling

Creative approach

Start from the meaning: a name rooted in scripture that also sounds like a modern event brand.

Carry one visual identity across posters, social posts and video so every touchpoint feels like the same event.
